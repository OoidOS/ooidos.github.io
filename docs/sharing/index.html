Docs

Sharing

Share files, your screen or a terminal with the computers and phones around you. Everything except Toss’s sending is off until you turn it on, in Settings › General › Sharing, and the firewall opens only what you turn on. Other computers find yours as yourcomputer.local.

Toss

Toss sends files to phones and computers nearby. It speaks LocalSend’s open protocol, so any device running LocalSend can send to OoidOS and receive from it.

  • Send: drop files on a device in Toss, the first Favourite in Files, or choose Share › Toss.
  • Receive: accept or decline each offer. Files arrive in Downloads.
  • Choose who can send to you: no one, your contacts, or everyone for ten minutes.

Screen Sharing

Let someone see and control your screen, even at the login window. Connect with any VNC viewer at vnc://yourcomputer.local, using your account’s name and password. On a Mac, use Share Screen… in the Finder. While someone is connected, the menu bar shows who, and you can disconnect them.

File Sharing

Share folders with the computers on your network, and choose who may read or change each one. Others connect at smb://yourcomputer.local.

To open folders shared by other computers, use Network in Files, or Go › Connect to Server (❖K).
