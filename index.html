Features

Why OoidOS

A desktop that stays out of your way, with the tools you reach for every day, all drawn as one.

One desktop, drawn as one

Every pixel, from window frames to the menu bar and dock, comes from one toolkit. Light or dark, in a look you choose, and easy to use from the keyboard.

Every window in view

Panorama shows all your windows at a glance. Spread your work across desktops, and tile windows side by side with a drag.

Apps you drag in and out

An app is a single bundle: drag it into Applications to install it, to the Bin to remove it. The Store brings in apps from Flathub.

Send to anything nearby

Toss drops files on phones and computers close by. Share your screen or folders whenever you choose to.

Go back in time

Rewind keeps hourly backups you can step through folder by folder, and Recovery repairs or reinstalls when you need it.

Private by default

Gatehouse shows every connection and can ask before an app reaches out. No telemetry, no account.

Get started

Try it in three steps

OoidOS runs straight from a USB stick, so you can try it before you install anything.

  1. Step 1

    Get the image

    The live USB image is coming soon. This page will link to it.

  2. Step 2

    Start from a USB stick

    Write the image to a stick and start your computer from it. Nothing on your disk changes.

  3. Step 3

    Explore, then install

    Look around in the live session, then install OoidOS on its own or beside your current system.

Open source

On its way to you

OoidOS is being prepared for an open-source release. Its code, its live image and its licence are coming together.

About licensing
  • Built from sourceEvery piece is built from its source code, and every source is pinned to an exact checksum.
  • Free software insideThe Linux kernel, wlroots, PipeWire, CUPS, Samba and many more, each under its own licence.
  • A licence on the wayOoidOS's own licence is being chosen as the code is prepared for release.
