What you get

Why OoidOS

The things you do every day, made simple.

Calm and tidy

A clean desktop that stays out of your way. Everything looks and works the same way, so there's less to learn. Pick a look you like, in light or dark.

Apps without the fuss

Popular apps like GIMP and Krita install from the Store, free and with no account needed. To remove an app, just drag it to the Bin.

Windows apps, through Wine

Many Windows apps run through Wine, set up as ordinary apps you open from the Dock. Affinity works, for one. It varies from app to app, and some need extra parts the first time. How it works

Updates you don't worry about

Updates install next to the system you're using. If a new version doesn't start properly, your computer goes back to the one that worked.

Your files, kept safe

Rewind backs up your files every hour, so you can go back to any folder as it was. If something goes really wrong, Recovery can fix or reinstall OoidOS.

Private, and easy to share

Nothing about you is sent anywhere: no account, no tracking. Toss sends files to phones and computers nearby, and sharing your screen or folders stays off until you turn it on.

Get started

Try it in three steps

Most PCs and laptops with an Intel or AMD processor can run it. Check yours.

  1. Step 1

    Get a USB stick

    The OoidOS image is coming soon. Write it to any USB stick of 2 GB or more.

  2. Step 2

    Start your computer from it

    OoidOS runs straight from the stick. Nothing on your computer changes.

  3. Step 3

    Look around, then decide

    Like it? Install OoidOS on its own, or next to the system you already have.

Ready to try OoidOS?

Start it from a USB stick, look around, and install it when you're ready.
