Desktop and apps

Four desktop looks

Pick Cupertino, Redmond, Coyoacán or Tübingen for the window layout and controls you like. Then choose the look’s own colours or the earthy OoidOS theme. Both come in light and dark.

See all four looks

Find apps in the Store

Get apps like GIMP and Krita without an account. Windows apps run through Wine, including Affinity.

Read about apps

Upgrade and keep your files

Start from an OoidOS USB stick and choose Upgrade, keeping your files. If a regular update fails to start, your computer goes back to the previous version on its own.

Read the upgrade steps

Notes, photos and recordings

Keep ideas and lists together in Notes. Take photos or record short videos with Camera.

  • Connect your devices

    Pair Bluetooth headphones, add a printer on your network or turn on remote access to use OoidOS from another computer. Toss sends files to phones and computers nearby.

  • Go back to an earlier file

    Set up Rewind for hourly backups. You can recover an earlier version or bring back a file you deleted.

Coming next

Automatic window tiling, bars and widgets you can build yourself, smoother graphics, and Notes and other apps on more systems.
